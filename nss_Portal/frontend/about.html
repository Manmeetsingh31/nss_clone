About NSS

National Service Scheme

Learn, serve and lead

NSS is designed around student participation in community service and experiential learning. This page intentionally uses general programme language; university-specific facts must be inserted only after verification.

Core themes include community service, personality development, leadership, social responsibility, national integration and volunteerism.

Objectives

Six programme objectives

Community Service

Participate in activities that address identified community needs.

Personality Development

Develop confidence, empathy and practical skills through service.

Leadership

Practice teamwork, organisation and responsible decision-making.

Social Responsibility

Build awareness of civic duties and community wellbeing.

National Integration

Encourage cooperation across communities and backgrounds.

Volunteerism

Promote a sustained culture of voluntary service.

Vision

Students who contribute with purpose

Build a culture where students learn through service and use their skills for constructive community engagement.

Mission

Service as experiential learning

Provide opportunities for volunteers to work collaboratively, reflect on community needs and develop leadership through action.
